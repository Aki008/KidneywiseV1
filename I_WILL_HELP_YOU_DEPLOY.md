## ✨ How to Get an API Key (5 minutes)

### For Claude (Recommended):

1. Go to: https://console.anthropic.com/
2. Click "Sign Up"
3. Enter your email
4. Verify email
5. Add payment method (credit card)
6. Go to "API Keys"
7. Click "Create Key"
8. Copy the key (starts with `sk-ant-`)
9. **Save it somewhere safe!**

### For OpenAI:

1. Go to: https://platform.openai.com/signup

3. Enter your email
4. Verify email
5. Add payment method
6. Go to "API Keys"
